## 🎮 About the Game

GunZ: The Duel is an online third-person action shooter developed by MAIET Entertainment. It first launched in South Korea in June 2003, with the North American edition released on November 29, 2006. The game combines fast-paced gunplay, melee combat, acrobatic movement, and role-playing elements.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **GunZ: The Duel**. This trainer provides configurable tools for offline practice, testing, and controlled private sessions. It works with GunZ mechanics such as health, armor, ammunition, reloading, weapon switching, melee attacks, dashing, jumping, tumbling, and wall-running.

**Target Audience:** Players, mod developers, and testers who want to practice movement, inspect weapon behavior, test combat values, or experiment in controlled GunZ environments.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

GunZ movement is built around directional running, jumping, dashing, tumbling, blocking, and temporary wall-running. Practice chaining jumps with directional dashes, then combine sword attacks and blocks to learn techniques such as Butterfly and other K-Style movement patterns. Use movement-related trainer options sparingly so timing and positioning remain easy to evaluate.

### Tools & Inventory

Prepare the character before entering a session by selecting armor, accessories, a melee weapon, and available firearms. GunZ combat commonly alternates between a sword or other melee weapon and ranged weapons. Test one loadout at a time to compare ammunition use, reload behavior, damage, and weapon-switch timing.

### World & Environment

GunZ is organized around lobbies, rooms, arenas, and mission areas rather than an open world. Use training rooms or private matches to test map routes, wall-running surfaces, jumps, cover, and vertical positions. Map layouts reward players who use walls and elevated angles instead of relying only on ground movement.

### Combat & Weapons

GunZ combines close-range melee attacks with firearms. Swords can slash, block incoming attacks, and support movement-cancel techniques, while guns provide sustained ranged pressure. Practice weapon switching, reload timing, aerial shots, dash attacks, and defensive blocks. Begin with normal damage and ammunition values, then enable individual trainer options when testing a specific mechanic.
